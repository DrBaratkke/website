Meet the Doctor

Dr. Elena Vance,
D.D.S., M.Sc.

"Dentistry is the intersection of medicine and art. Every treatment is designed not just for health, but for harmony and longevity."

Trained in the rigorous standards of Swiss academies and refined through over 15 years of practice in Zurich, New York, and London, Dr. Vance brings a unique global perspective to her clinic.

She specializes in minimally invasive restorative procedures, believing that preserving the natural tooth structure is the ultimate sophistication. Her clinic is a sanctuary for the discerning patient who values precision, privacy, and an unhurried atmosphere.

Logistics & Planning FAQ

How long should I plan to stay? expand_more
Most major treatments require a stay of 3-5 days. For full mouth restorations or implants, two visits separated by a healing period might be necessary. We will provide a detailed timeline during your initial consultation.
Do you accept international insurance? expand_more
We work with many major international insurance providers. Our administrative team will assist you in verifying coverage and preparing all necessary claim forms for reimbursement.
Can I bring a companion? expand_more
Absolutely. We can arrange accommodation and transport for your companion as well. Our concierge can also suggest local activities for them while you are undergoing treatment.
Is financing available for international patients? expand_more
We offer various payment plans and financing options through our banking partners. Please contact our financial coordinator for specific details relevant to your country of residence.
